A club runs the auction; this site is where it happens. You join, add lots and bid until bidding ends at a set time. Then you pay the club, and everybody meets once to hand over bags.

There's no live session to watch. Every lot is up for the whole auction, and you can bid any time.

1. Join the auction

Signing up for the site and joining an auction are two different things. You need both. Without joining, there's no bid button and no way to add lots.

  1. Sign in. {% ui "Sign in with Google" %} or {% ui "Sign in with Apple" %} is one click. Confirmation email not arriving? See Your account.
  2. Open {% page "auction_main" "the auction's page" %}. Your club's link goes straight there, or find it under {% page "auctions" "Auctions" %} at the top of the page.
  3. Read the rules, then scroll right to the bottom.
  4. Tick Yes, I will be at this auction and pick your pickup location.
  5. Click Join auction.

The pickup location is where you collect what you win, and drop off what you sell. You can change it at the bottom of the auction page until bidding ends.

The list of auctions shows the ones near you first; click {% ui "Starts" %} to sort by date. {% page "all_auctions" "Every auction" %}, past ones too, is there as well.

2. Add your lots

  1. On the auction page, click {% ui "Add Lot" %}. (It's also {% page "new_lot" "Create new lot" %} on the {% page "selling" "Selling" %} page.)
  2. The first time, the site asks for your name, address and phone. The address is where the club mails your check.
  3. Name it: short, and say what it is. "Pair of Apistogramma cacatuoides". Leave numbers out; the lot number is added for you.
  4. Pick the species from the list if it's alive. The category fills itself in.
  5. The rest is optional: quantity, description, a minimum price, a buy now price, {% ui "I bred this" %}.
  6. Save. A green banner says the lot was created.
  7. {% ui "Add photos" %}: {% page "add_image" "Add image" %} on the lot's page, straight from your phone.

What sellers ask

How big a lot?Smaller. Split $100 of shrimp into several bags: the cheaper the lot, the more people bid on it.
Minimum or buy now?A minimum means it won't sell for less. A buy now price close to what it's worth sells straight away; a silly one just means it goes to auction. Starting high and lowering it later doesn't work: people look once.
Editing{% page "edit_lot" "Edit lot" %} works until somebody bids or lot submission ends. After that, ask the people running the auction.
Deleting{% page "delete_lot" "Delete" %} works until somebody bids. With an unsold lot fee, only in the first day after you add the lot, and not in the last day of lot submission.
Odd formattingA description pasted from a website brings its formatting along. Paste it into Notepad first, then copy it from there.
A videoPut a YouTube link in the reference link box; it plays next to the photos.
The wrong speciesFix it on the lot page, or pick "No species" for equipment.

The {% page "selling" "Selling" %} page lists your lots, with views, bids and unread questions. {% page "my_lot_report" "Export my sold lots CSV" %} downloads them. {% page "messages" "Chat notification setup" %} picks which lots email you when somebody asks a question.

3. Bid

Open {% page "lot_by_pk" "a lot" %}, type the most you'd pay, click {% ui "Place bid" %}, and confirm. You don't pay what you typed. You pay just enough to beat the next person.

  • Each bid must beat the price by 5%, and by at least $1.
  • Raise your maximum any time. You can't lower it below the current price.
  • If nobody else bids, you get it at the opening price.
  • Only you can see your maximum.
  • A price typed in the lot's chat isn't a bid.

Click {% ui "Watch" %}, or the star in the lot list, on anything you like. You get an email two hours before it ends. The {% page "watched" "Buying" %} page lists what you're {% page "watched" "watching" %}, {% page "my_bids" "bidding on" %} and {% page "won_lots" "have won" %}. {% page "my_won_lot_csv" "Export CSV" %} downloads it.

Finding lots

  • {% ui "View Lots" %} on the auction page shows this auction. {% page "allLots" "Lots" %} at the top of the page shows every auction, which is why you'll see other clubs' lots there.
  • Sold lots are hidden unless you pick {% ui "Open/ended" %}. Your lot didn't vanish; it sold.
  • {% ui "Least popular" %}, or {% ui "Only unviewed" %}, finds the lots nobody's looked at.
  • {% page "user_lots" "All lots from" %} a seller, on any lot page, shows what else they're selling.
  • Something that shouldn't be for sale? {% page "report_lot" "Report" %} it.

Don't want your name next to your bids? Untick {% ui "Username visible" %} in your preferences. The people running the auction still see who you are.

4. How an auction ends

Each lot ends at the auction's end time, unless somebody bids in the last 15 minutes. Then that lot runs 15 more minutes from the bid, and again for each late bid, up to an hour past the end.{% if help.site.online_extended %} About {{ help.site.online_extended }}% of sold lots on this site end late that way.{% endif %}

{% tip %}Waiting until the last second doesn't work here. Put in your real maximum early and go to bed.{% endtip %}

There's no countdown clock, on purpose. The exact end time is on each lot's page. Hover over a bid to see when it came in.

5. Your invoice, and paying it

Your invoice is ready when the auction ends: {% page "my_auction_invoice" "My Invoice" %} on the auction page, or {% page "my_invoices" "Invoices" %} in the menu under your name. You get an email with a link once the club has checked the invoices.

It nets out what you bought against what you sold. Sold $135, bought $20? The club owes you $115 and you pay nothing. Totals are rounded to the dollar in your favour.

How you pay is up to the club. The invoice says how:

  • A Pay now button: pay by card, and the invoice marks itself paid.
  • A PayPal invoice, sent separately. You don't need a PayPal account; a card works. Check your spam folder.
  • Cash or check at pickup.

Errors like "this merchant is restricted" come from the club's payment account, not yours. Tell the club.

When your invoice is paid, you get an email thanking you for being part of the auction. It's your receipt, not a bill. {% page "invoice_by_pk" "Print invoice" %}, on the invoice, prints it.

6. Pickup day

Your invoice has everything: the location, the time, a {% ui "Get directions" %} button, and who to call if you're late. {% page "add_to_calendar" "Add to calendar" %}, on the auction page, puts it in your calendar.

  • Be on time. Fish sit in bags while everyone waits.
  • Somebody else can pick up for you. Give them the name on your invoice, and make sure it's paid.
  • Didn't win anything? You don't need to come.

Two times on your invoice? Lots are being driven between locations. The first time is for lots from sellers at your own location, the second for lots from the others.

The club pays you after pickup, usually by check to the address on your account, within a week or two.

When something's wrong with a lot

Dead on arrival, short, not as described, or it never turned up: tell the people running the auction, not the seller. Reply to any email you got about the auction, or use the {% ui "Email" %} button on the auction page. Send the lot number.

They'll refund some or all of it through the invoices. It comes off the seller's payment, and the club gives up its cut too.

{% tip %}Don't sort out money with the seller yourselves. A seller who sends you $5 for a dead fish still pays the club's cut on the sale, and the club may refund you as well.{% endtip %}
