Your member list is everyone in the club: when they joined, when they last paid, and when they run out. Members can pay their own dues by card and carry their membership card on their phone. Or skip all that and just keep the list.

For members

  • Joining: on the club's {% page "club_detail" "page" %}, click {% ui "Join" %}. No button means the club adds members itself; ask them.
  • Your card: {% ui "Membership" %} on the club page shows your membership number and its barcode, when it runs out, and a button to put it in your phone's wallet. It updates itself when you renew.
  • Paying: if the club takes dues here, the same panel has a button to {% page "club_membership_pay" "pay or renew" %} by card. You're renewed when the payment goes through.
  • Club emails: the link at the bottom of any membership email picks all emails, essential ones only (membership and payment notices), or none.

Belong to a club that does breeder awards? See Breeder award programs.

Importing a spreadsheet

  1. Save it as a CSV. Name the columns: Email (required), First Name, Last Name, Phone, Address, Memo, Discord ID, Contact Status, Membership Last Paid, Membership Expiration Date, Date Joined and Send Welcome Letter. Leave out any you don't have.
  2. Click {% page "club_member_import" "Import from CSV" %}, pick the file, and {% ui "Upload CSV" %}.
  3. Check the preview. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.
  • Somebody already on the list, by email, is updated from the file.
  • Dates can be partial: "2025" means January 1st, 2025.
  • People you import don't get the welcome letter unless their Send Welcome Letter says yes.

The same person twice

It happens: one entry from the old spreadsheet and one from signing up. A next to a name means the site thinks it's found one. Search "duplicate" to see them all. Open the menu on the one you don't want, click {% page "club_member_merge" "Merge with..." %}, and pick the one to keep. Anything the kept one is missing is filled in from the other, the two sets of permissions are combined, and the other is deactivated. It can't be undone.

Dues

On {% page "club_membership_settings" "Membership and payments" %}:

{% ui "Membership system" %}{% ui "No membership fees" %}: nobody pays and nothing expires. {% ui "January 1st renewal" %}: everyone runs out on January 1st. {% ui "Rolling annual membership" %}: a year from the day each person pays.
{% ui "Membership annual fee" %}What a year costs.
{% ui "Show member barcodes" %}Gives every member a card with a barcode: see below.

Renewing before a membership runs out adds a year to it, so nobody loses anything by paying early.

{% mike %}Mike joined in December at a club with January 1st renewal. On New Year's Eve he got an email saying his membership expires tomorrow, and he's been telling everyone the club stole his $20. Set a December joiner's expiration to the end of next year by hand.{% endmike %}

Taking dues

  • By card: connect Square on the same page, and members pay on your club page. They're renewed when the payment goes through, and it's in the treasurer's report. See Club money.
  • Cash or check: {% ui "Renew" %} in the member's menu. It adds one membership period by your club's rules, records the dues in the treasurer's report, and sends the renewal email if you've turned it on.
  • Fixing a date: {% page "club_member_renew_page" "Set expiration date" %} changes the date and records no money. For somebody who has never paid, it also sends any welcome letter held back for them.
  • At an auction: with {% rule "add_membership_fee_to_invoices_for_expired_members" %} in an auction's rules, a lapsed member's invoice includes the fee, and paying it renews them.
{% tip %}Took money? {% ui "Renew" %}. Just correcting a mistake? {% ui "Set expiration date" %}. Using the wrong one either counts dues nobody paid, or loses dues somebody did.{% endtip %}

Your own membership form, on your website or Google Forms, doesn't make someone a paid member here. Renew them by hand, or have the form do it with an API key that can renew memberships.

Automatic renewals

Clubs that take PayPal can let members pay by yearly subscription, so their membership renews itself with nobody chasing it. Under {% page "club_membership_settings" "Membership and payments" %}, open {% ui "PayPal membership subscriptions" %}, follow the steps in PayPal, and paste the {% ui "PayPal webhook ID" %} it gives you.

Without PayPal, turn on the expiration reminders: once you charge a fee and have a payment account connected, each one carries a button to pay.

Membership cards

With {% ui "Show member barcodes" %} on, every member gets a number and a barcode. It's made by the site; you can't bring your own numbering.

  • Members find it under {% ui "Membership" %} on your club page, in their welcome and renewal emails, and in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet on their phone. The wallet card updates itself when they renew.
  • Scan it at the door to check them in, or at checkout to find their invoice. Barcodes and scanners has how.
  • {% ui "Resend membership card" %} emails it to them again.
  • A card was lost or shared? {% ui "Membership number" %}, then {% ui "Reset" %}. The old number stops working; resend the card.
  • No phone? Print it: add a row for them on {% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %} and click {% page "club_barcode_labels_pdf" "Download PDF" %}.
