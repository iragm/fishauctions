A club here has a public page, a member list, and its auctions. It can also take dues, send announcements, keep a calendar, run a breeder award program and track donations. Use as much or as little as you like: plenty of clubs only run auctions.

Being a member

{% page "clubs" "Find a club" %}, under {% ui "Clubs" %} at the top of the page, shows clubs on a map and in a list. Filter for ones with something coming up, or taking new members.

Each club has a {% page "club_detail" "page" %} with its next events, its latest announcement, and links to its website, Facebook and Discord. If it takes members through the site, there's a {% ui "Join" %} button. Once you're in, {% ui "Membership" %} on that page has your card, and a button to pay your dues. Members and dues has the rest.

{% tip %}Your club isn't listed? Anyone can get in touch to have it added.{% endtip %}

Setting up your club

  1. Get in touch with the club's name and website, and who should run it here. We add it.
  2. Open the club's menu: the button with your club's name, on the left.
  3. {% page "club_setup" "Setup" %} lists every settings page, with a line on what each is for.
  4. Start with {% page "club_edit" "General" %}.
{% ui "Name" %}The club's name is also its page's link, so changing it changes the link. Do it early.
{% ui "Icon" %}Your logo, square. It goes on the club page, your emails and members' wallet cards.
{% ui "Homepage" %}, {% ui "Facebook page" %}, {% ui "Discord invite link" %}Each one is a button on your club page.
{% ui "Allow joining" %}Puts a {% ui "Join" %} button on your club page. Anyone signed in can click it and they're on your member list.
{% ui "Enable breeder award program" %}Turns on points and leaderboards. See Breeder award programs.
{% ui "About this club" %}A paragraph at the top of your club page: when you meet, and why somebody should come.
{% ui "Location" %} and {% ui "Map" %}Where you meet. Type the address and drag the pin. This is how the club finder and the member map know where you are.
{% ui "Mailing address" %}The law wants a postal address on every email a club sends, so the club can't send any until this is filled in. It's also where vendors mail donations.

Who can do what

Everyone who helps run the club is on the member list with their own permissions. On {% page "club_admin" "Members" %}, open the menu at the end of a member's row, click {% ui "Permissions" %}, and tick what they need:

View membersSee the member list.
Manage membershipAdd, edit and renew members. {% ui "CSV import/export" %} is separate.
Manage membership and paymentsDues, the club's card payments, and the treasurer's report.
Manage auctionsRun the club's auctions.
Award pointsThe breeder award program.
Manage donationsAdd and email donation vendors.
Send announcementsPost to Discord, the app, email and the club page.
Edit club settingsSetup, Discord and API keys. Nearly as powerful as admin.
Club adminEverything, including giving other people permissions. Give it to as few people as you can.

So your BAP chair can approve points without touching auctions, and your membership chair can add members without seeing the money.

{% mike %}Mike was made club admin in 2019, "just for the auction". He still is. Last week he tidied up the member list and deactivated the treasurer. Give people the permission their job needs, and take it back when the job's done.{% endmike %}

Club auctions

An auction belongs to the club when you pick the club in its rules. Anyone with {% ui "Manage auctions" %} can run it, and it takes payments into the club's account.

Set {% ui "Manage users through club" %} in the rules and the auction's people are your member list:

  • Members keep their club bidder number at every auction.
  • A new person at the door becomes a club member.
  • Edit somebody once, and it's changed everywhere.
  • With check-in on, nobody can bid until they've been checked in.
  • The rules can add your membership fee to the invoice of anyone whose membership has lapsed, and renew them when they pay.

Your club page lists your auctions. {% ui "Make current" %} next to one pins it: the club's menu then has shortcuts for auction day, {% ui "Add user" %}, {% ui "Set lot winners" %}, {% ui "Checkin" %}, {% ui "Checkout" %} and {% ui "Print labels" %}, and the auction snippet on your website shows it. Running an in-person auction explains each one.

{% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %} also prints barcodes for things you sell at the desk. Scan the T-shirt, then the person's card, and it's on their invoice. Barcodes and scanners has the rest.

After an auction, {% page "auction_add_users_to_club" "Add all users to your club" %} on the Users tab puts everyone from it on your member list.

The library

The {% page "library" "Library" %}, under {% ui "Clubs" %}, keeps old newsletters, BAP reports and articles where people can search them and ask them questions. Upload a whole pile of page photos or scans at once: they're read, put back together into one document per article, and filed by subject and by the species they name. Choose who can read them: everyone on the site, only your club's members, or only you. Whoever uploaded a document, or can edit the club, can correct what was read off it.
