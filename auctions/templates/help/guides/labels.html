What labels are for

Labels are optional; a lot number in marker works too. But printed labels are faster at the desk, easier to read across a room, and carry a QR code. Scan it with any phone camera and the lot's page opens, with the seller, the winner and the price.

Printing labels for your own lots

In person: before you come

  1. Add all your lots first, so they fit on as few sheets as possible.
  2. On the auction's page, click {% page "print_my_labels" "Print Labels" %}. You get a PDF, one label per lot.
  3. Tape each label to its bag with clear packing tape.

A label got wet or went missing? Open the lot and click {% page "single_lot_label" "Print label" %}. If a lot sells early at its buy now price, you'll get an email with a link that {% page "print_my_unprinted_labels" "prints only the labels that need printing" %}.

Online: after bidding ends

Only lots that sold need one. Click {% ui "Print labels for your lots" %} on your invoice. You'll get an email when it's time.

{% tip %}"You don't have permission" when you print? You're signed in with a different account from the one your lots are under, often on a second device. Sign out and back in.{% endtip %}

Printing everybody's labels

If you run the auction, you can print anybody's:

One person{% ui "Print labels" %} next to their name on the {% page "auction_tos_list" "Users" %} tab. {% page "print_labels_by_bidder_number" "That page" %} prints all their labels; there's a {% page "print_unprinted_labels_by_bidder_number" "version for just the unprinted ones" %}. Quickest at the desk, as each seller arrives.
A few, or everyone{% page "auction_printing" "Print labels" %} under {% ui "More" %}. Tick the people and click print. You get {% page "auction_printing_pdf" "one PDF" %}.

The site remembers which labels have been printed, by anyone. Leave {% ui "Only print labels that haven't been printed yet" %} ticked and nothing prints twice. To print everything again, untick it.

One PDF holds up to 100 labels on a thermal printer, or 500 on sheets. More than that? Print again with "only unprinted" ticked for the next batch.

Sheets, thermal printers and the phone app

What you print on is your own setting, on {% page "printing" "Label printing" %}. One admin can use a thermal printer and another a laser printer on the same night.

SettingGood for
Plain paperAny printer. Cut them out and tape them on. Fine for a few lots.
Large (Avery 18262)Sheet labels on a laser printer. Inkjet ink runs when the bag gets wet.
Small (Avery 5160)Cheap and easy to find, but hard to read. Not recommended.
Thermal 3"x2"The one to buy if you're buying a printer: fast, no ink, and it takes water. Any thermal printer that takes 3"x2" labels works.
Thermal 1⅛" x 3½" (Dymo 30252)Dymo LabelWriters.
CustomYour own page and label sizes. Test a few first: problems show up on the fifth label, not the first.
  • A half-used sheet? Set {% ui "Empty labels" %} to the number already gone. Set it back to 0 for a fresh sheet.
  • A Bluetooth thermal printer prints straight from the phone app, no computer. Pick it on Label printing in the app, and every print button sends labels to it.
  • Trying a setting? Reprint labels from an old auction you sold in.

What goes on a label

The people running an auction choose, at {% page "auction_label_config" "Set what gets printed on labels" %} (on the Print labels page). It applies to every label printed for that auction, sellers' own included.

{% if help.label_fields %}{% helptip %}Labels in {{ help.auction.title }} print: {{ help.label_fields|join:", " }}.{% endhelptip %}{% endif %}
ChoiceNotes
Lot numberAlways.
Lot name, QR CodeOn by default. The QR code opens the lot's page.
Seller's nameOn by default, while the lot is unsold.
Minimum bid, Buy now priceOnly on lots that have one, so the auctioneer notices.
Scientific name, Quantity, Donation, Breeder pointsOn by default, where the lot has one.
Your custom fieldsA table number, "CARES", which club brought it.
Category, Auction dateOff by default. The date helps people remember when they bought a fish.
Seller's email, DescriptionOff, and best left off. Descriptions are long, and the club should sort out problems, not the buyer and seller.

Once a lot is sold, its label shows the winner's name instead of the seller's, and no prices. With more than one pickup location, it also says where the lot is going.

Long lot names get cut short. Put the detail in the description or a custom field: "Aphyosemion australe" in the name, the collection code in a custom field.

When labels come out wrong

On the wrong part of the sheetMost sheets have different margins top and bottom. Turn the sheet around.
An edge cut offTry Fit to page instead of Actual size in the print dialog, or the other way round.
Labels creep, skip, or come out page-sizedSet the printer to the label size, not Letter or A4, and recalibrate. Still skipping? Try another roll; cheap rolls are the usual cause.
Faint lines across thermal labelsClean the print head. If the lines are in the PDF on screen too, tell us.
Tiny, or the wrong sizeCheck {% page "printing" "Label printing" %} matches the paper, on the account you're signed in with.
The page times out on a big batchPrint per person from the Users tab.
