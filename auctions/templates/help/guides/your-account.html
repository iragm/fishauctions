Signing in

{% ui "Sign in with Google" %} or {% ui "Sign in with Apple" %} is quickest: one click, no password. Otherwise click {% ui "Create account" %} and use your email.

After you sign up, the site emails you a link. Click it: that proves the email is yours. Replying "yes, that's me" doesn't do anything.

If the link doesn't work

  • Check your spam folder, and that you typed your email right.
  • Paste the link into your browser's address bar, not a search box.
  • Links only last a few hours. Sign in, and the site sends a fresh one.
  • Try another browser or device.

"An account already exists with this email" means you've signed up before. Sign in, or click Forgot your password?. The reset goes to the email on the account, which may not be the one you check most.

Watched lots missing, can't print labels, invoice won't open? Check the name at the top right. You're probably signed in with a different account on this device.

Want Google or Apple sign-in on an existing account? Sign in the usual way, then use {% ui "Connect Google/Apple account" %} in your account pages. Signing in with Google under a different email makes a second account instead.

Ended up with two anyway? Get in touch and we'll merge them.

Your name, address and phone

{% page "contact_info" "Contact info" %} holds your real name, phone and mailing address.

  • {% ui "Your name" %} goes on your invoices. Only the people running auctions you join see it.
  • {% ui "Your address" %} is where clubs mail your check when you sell. Keep it up to date.
  • The map pin is how the site knows what's near you.
  • Change it once and it changes in every club and auction you're in.

Your {% ui "username" %} is public: it's on your lots, bids and chat messages, for anyone to see. Don't use your email or full name. {% page "change_username" "Change username" %} changes it. You can sign in with either your username or your email.

To use a different email, go to {% ui "Change email" %}. Only you can change it. It doesn't need to match your PayPal: any card can pay any invoice.

Notifications

{% page "notification_preferences" "Notifications" %} is when the site may contact you:

  • {% ui "Auctions near you" %}, online and in person: one message per auction, only if you haven't been on the site for a few days. Set how far "near" is.
  • Questions on your lots, and replies to yours.
  • A reminder when you look at an auction but don't join.
  • {% ui "Push notifications when lots sell" %}: at an in-person auction, a notification when a lot you're watching comes up. Works in the phone app, or in a web browser after you click {% ui "Subscribe to Push Messaging" %} on that device. How they work.
  • With the phone app: notifications instead of emails, and a running total of what you've bought.

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. {% page "messages" "Chat notification setup" %} picks which lots' questions you hear about.

Emails from auctions

When you join an auction, add lots, or your invoice is ready, the site emails you on behalf of the people running it.

{% tip %}Reply to any of those emails and your reply goes to them: the club, or whoever set the auction up. It's the quickest way to ask about drop-off, pickup, or a lot with a problem.{% endtip %}

Questions about the website itself go to the contact page.

Your data, and deleting your account

  • {% page "account_data_export" "Download my data" %}: one file with everything the site holds about you.
  • {% page "account_delete" "Delete account" %}: says exactly what goes and what stays. Your sign-in and profile go; auction results stay, without your name, so other people's invoices still add up. It happens 30 days later, and signing in before then cancels it.
  • {% page "account" "My account" %}: your public page as you see it, with links to all of the above.
