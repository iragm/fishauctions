{% load help_tags %} {% comment %}See HELP.md for the voice. The treasurer's side of a club: the report, the ledger behind it, and the club's payment accounts. The ledger is ClubMoney, booked from paid invoices by Invoice.sync_club_money (cash basis).{% endcomment %}

Auction sales, seller payouts, dues and sales tax are recorded here by themselves as invoices are paid. You add everything else: hall rent, a speaker's fee, a raffle. The treasurer's report adds it up for any dates you like.

{% tip %}It's a record, not accounting software. Check it against the bank.{% endtip %}

Below that is every record in the date range, and the club's current balance.

What's recorded by itself

Money is recorded when it changes hands, which here means when an invoice is marked {% ui "Paid" %}:

  • A buyer's invoice: their payment, and any sales tax on it.
  • A seller's invoice, where the club owes them: their payout. So mark it paid when you mail the check, not before.
  • Dues, whether a member paid online, you clicked {% ui "Renew" %}, or it was on their auction invoice.

Unmark an invoice and its records are reversed. Nothing is ever deleted, so the report always adds up.

{% mike %}Mike, as treasurer, marked every seller paid on auction night "to keep things tidy", then mailed the checks three weeks later. For three weeks the report said the club had $2,000 less than the bank did.{% endmike %} {% aitip %}Ask "what's our balance?"{% endaitip %}

Everything else

  • {% page "club_money_add" "Add record" %}: money in or out that isn't an invoice. A negative {% ui "Amount" %} is an expense. Pick a {% ui "Category" %}: {% ui "Donation" %}, {% ui "Speaker costs" %}, {% ui "Meeting location cost" %}, {% ui "Refund" %}.
  • {% page "club_money_balance" "Balance books" %}: when you start, type what's actually in the bank, and the difference is recorded as one {% ui "Balance adjustment" %}. Needing it twice means something is going unrecorded.
  • {% page "club_treasurer_report_export" "Export CSV" %}: the records in the date range, for QuickBooks or your accountant.
{% aitip %}Ask "put $40 in for the raffle prizes". If your accounting software has a connector, connect it too and ask "copy this month's hall rent and speaker fees from QuickBooks into the club's records", or "what's in our books that isn't in the report?"{% endaitip %}

The club's payment accounts

With a card account connected, members pay their dues on your club page and every club auction gets a pay button. It's on {% page "club_membership_settings" "Membership and payments" %}, under Setup.

  • Square, for any club: {% ui "Connect a Square account for this club" %}. {% page "club_link_payment_account" "Linking" %} uses the Square account of the person clicking, so the treasurer should do it. Already connected Square for your own auctions? {% ui "Use my connected Square account" %}.
    • {% if help.club_own_paypal %}
  • PayPal: the club enters its own {% page "club_paypal_credentials" "PayPal credentials" %} on the same page.
    • {% endif %}
  • The first time, card payments have to be switched on for you. Click {% ui "Contact us and request access" %}.

The money goes straight into that account; this site takes nothing. Card payments has the details, Tap to Pay, and why there's no button to pay sellers.
