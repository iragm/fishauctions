Square

{% square_account %}

Connecting it

For a club

  1. Open the club's {% page "club_membership_settings" "Membership and payments" %}.
  2. Click {% ui "Connect a Square account for this club" %} and sign in to Square.

It uses the Square account of whoever clicks, so the treasurer should do it. Dues and every club auction then use it.

For an auction with no club

  1. Sign in here, and click {% ui "Connect your Square account" %} at the top of this section.
  2. Sign in to Square.

Your auctions take payments into your own account.

Then tick {% ui "Square payments" %} in your auction's rules. It only shows once an account is connected. A club auction uses the club's account, or the account of whoever created the auction if the club hasn't connected one.

Square works in the US and Canada. You can have one Square account connected at a time. {% page "square_seller_delete" "Disconnect" %} it any time.

When people pay

Online auctions

  • The pay button appears an hour after bidding ends, once late bids have finished.
  • Set invoices to {% ui "Ready" %} to email everyone a link.
  • Paid invoices mark themselves paid.

In person

  • {% page "auction_quick_checkout" "Checkout" %} shows a QR code for each person.
  • They scan it and pay on their own phone. It takes about ten seconds to come through.
  • No QR code? The pay button is on their invoice too.
  • {% ui "Refunds" %} you make in Square show on the invoice by themselves.
  • Somebody paid cash instead? Mark the invoice paid yourself, and write "cash" in its {% ui "Memo" %}.
  • Long lines? Several admins can run checkout at once, on different phones.

Tap to Pay

With the phone app and Square connected, checkout has a {% ui "Tap to Pay" %} button. The buyer taps their card or phone on yours, and the invoice is paid. No card reader. On an iPhone it's called Tap to Pay on iPhone.

Connected Square before Tap to Pay existed? Reconnect it once to turn it on. Your settings stay as they are.

PayPal

{% paypal_account %}

Most clubs don't have PayPal here; Square is the one to use. A few clubs have it, set up by us. Their treasurer enters the club's own PayPal credentials, a client ID and secret from a PayPal app, on {% page "club_membership_settings" "Membership and payments" %}. After that it works like Square: a pay button on invoices and a QR code at checkout, with no Tap to Pay.

Want it for your club? Get in touch. Connected a PayPal account of your own in the past? It's shown above, and you can {% page "paypal_seller_delete" "disconnect" %} it.

Sending PayPal invoices yourself

No pay button? You can still bill everyone through PayPal after an online auction, from any PayPal account:

  1. On the auction's Users tab, fix up any invoices, then click {% ui "Set open invoices to ready" %}.
  2. Click {% ui "Export" %}, then {% ui "PayPal Invoice CSV" %}. You get a file of everyone who owes money.
  3. Click {% ui "PayPal Batch Invoice Upload" %} in the same menu and sign in to PayPal.
  4. Upload the file, check the invoices, and send them.
  5. PayPal doesn't tell this site who paid. Mark people paid yourself.

Paying sellers

This site doesn't pay sellers, and it never will.

  • Connecting Square lets the people running your auctions take payments, and refund ones made through an invoice. That's close to impossible to misuse.
  • A payout button would let any of them send the club's money anywhere: add a user, add a lot for them, mark it sold for $1,000, pay out.
  • Sellers can't take or ask for their payout here either.

Pay them from the {% ui "Users CSV" %}, under {% ui "Export" %} on the Users tab. Filter the tab by {% ui "Club owes" %} first and the file has only the people the club owes, with the amount in {% ui "Invoice total due" %}. It imports into PayPal, QuickBooks, or whatever your treasurer uses.
