Scanning saves typing at the three busiest places in an auction: the door, the auctioneer's table, and checkout. You can scan with a phone's camera, a USB barcode scanner, or both.

What you can scan

The codeWhere it comes fromScanning it
Lot QR codeEvery lot labelOpens the lot's page on any phone. On the lot queue, adds the lot to the queue.
Membership cardThe club's member barcodes, on phones, in Wallet, or printedAdds the member to the auction and checks them in. At checkout, pulls up their invoice.
Bidder paddle{% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %}Gives the next person you scan that bidder number.
Item for sale, or discount{% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %}Adds the charge or discount to the next person you scan.

Membership cards, paddles and items are for club auctions, where the club has member barcodes turned on. Lot QR codes work everywhere.

With a phone camera

These pages have an Activate camera button:

  • {% page "auction_quick_check_in" "Checkin" %}: scan membership cards at the door.
  • {% page "auction_quick_checkout" "Checkout" %}: scan a card to pull up that person's invoice.
  • {% page "auction_lot_queue" "Lot queue" %}: scan each bag's label as it comes to the table.

A lot's QR code needs no page at all: any phone camera opens the lot, with its seller, winner and price.

{% tip %}Camera won't start on an iPhone? In Safari, tap aA in the address bar, Website Settings, and set Camera to Allow. It doesn't work inside another app's built-in browser; open the page in Safari or Chrome. The Checkin page has a Camera not working? section to send us if it still won't.{% endtip %}

With a USB scanner

A USB scanner is faster than a camera at a busy door. It types the barcode into the computer like a keyboard, and every admin page of a club auction listens for it. Nothing needs to be clicked first.

Any cheap "keyboard wedge" scanner works, if you can set it to send F9 before each scan and Enter after. The F9 is how the site tells a scan from somebody typing. The scanner's manual has setup barcodes for both.

  1. Open {% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %} in your club's menu.
  2. Click Test scanner and scan the test barcode. It says whether the F9 and Enter are set up right.

Every scan beeps, with a message on screen: two high beeps when it worked, one when it's waiting for the next scan (a paddle, say, waiting for its person), and a low one when it didn't work.

Printing barcodes

{% page "club_barcode_labels" "Print Barcodes" %} makes a sheet of barcodes on your label printer. Add a row for each:

Bidder paddleA bidder number. Scan the paddle, then the person, and that's their number.
Item for saleAn amount and a name: "$20 Shirt". Scan it, then the person, and it's on their invoice.
DiscountThe same, taken off.
UserA member's card, for someone without a phone.

The page doesn't save anything. Check the sheet printed right before you leave it.

{% tip %}Print a sheet of your T-shirts, raffle tickets and membership renewals, laminate it, and keep it at the checkout table. It works every month.{% endtip %}

Charges only go on an open invoice. Somebody who's already paid gets a message instead.

A self check-in kiosk

{% page "auction_self_check_in" "Self-checkin" %}, under More, turns a laptop with a scanner into a kiosk. Members scan their own card and get a welcome on the screen. It only takes membership cards, so nobody can scan a T-shirt onto their own invoice.
