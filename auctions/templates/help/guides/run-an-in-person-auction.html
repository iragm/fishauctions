How a club auction runs

{% tip %}Only the people running the auction need the site. Bidders don't need an account, or even an email address.{% endtip %}

Before the day

Sellers add their own lots and print their own labels. You add the people you know are coming.

At the door

Look each person up, hand them a bidder number, and add any lots they brought.

The auction

As each lot sells, a bid recorder types the lot number, the price and the winner's bidder number.

Checkout

Look the person up. Their invoice says what they owe or are owed. Take the money, click Paid.

Who does what

AnnouncerCalls the lots. Big auctions swap announcers every hour or so. Not every auction has a separate announcer.
AuctioneerYou know what this is: "$10 going once, $10 going twice, sold!"
RunnersBring each bag from the table to the auctioneer, hold it up so the room can see it, and hand it to the winner. With the lot queue, a runner scans each bag as it comes up.
Door or registration deskChecks people in and hands out bidder numbers. Adds walk-ins, and lots nobody pre-registered.
CheckoutTakes the money. Several people can run checkout at once, on phones.
TreasurerPays sellers afterwards, and keeps the books.
Bid recordersSit next to the auctioneer with a laptop and type in each sale. Need to be quick and careful.
{% tip %}Two bid recorders can double check each other's work, reducing typos to nearly 0.{% if help.site.double_checked is not None %} {{ help.site.double_checked }}% of all lots have been double checked, but you'll do better in your auctions, right? Right?{% endif %}{% endtip %}

A monthly meeting auction needs two people and a laptop. A big annual auction needs two or three laptops at the door and at checkout, a printer, two fast bid recorders, a treasurer, a couple of announcers or auctioneers, and plenty of runners.

Why everyone pays at the end

Some clubs have buyers pay cash for each lot as it's won. It's slower and it goes wrong:

  • The auction waits while somebody makes change, 200 times.
  • Every payment is a chance to take the wrong amount, or none.
  • Sellers still have to be paid, so somebody adds it all up afterwards anyway.
  • A seller who also bought pays and gets paid separately, instead of the difference.

With checkout at the end, every invoice is already added up. Each person pays once, or is paid once, and it takes seconds.

{% tip %}Run a practice auction of 20 or 30 lots with your helpers before the real one. Everybody learns their job, and you find out whether the hall's wifi works.{% endtip %}

1. Create the auction

  1. Click {% ui "Auctions" %} at the top of the page, then {% page "auction_confirm" "Create a new auction" %}, then {% page "create_auction" "Create a club auction" %}.
  2. Pick the date and start time, and a name. The name becomes the link and can't be changed, so get the year right.
  3. Click {% ui "Create in-person auction" %}. Done this before? {% ui "Copy" %} your last auction instead: same rules and location, new date.
  4. Under {% ui "More" %}, click {% page "auction_pickup_location" "Location" %} and put the hall on the map. Add parking and which door to use to the description.

The {% ui "Finish setting up" %} box on your auction page ticks off what's done. First auction? Click {% ui "Contact us and request access" %} there before you can promote it or have invoices emailed.

{% tip %}Put the auction's link on your flyer as a QR code. Check it scans before you print.{% endtip %} {% aitip %}Ask "set up the spring auction". It copies last year's.{% endaitip %}

2. Set the rules

Click {% page "edit_auction" "Rules" %} under {% ui "More" %}. What the auction rules do covers every setting. The ones that matter most in person:

  • {% ui "Bidding opens" %} is the time on your flyer. Doors at 9, bidding at 11? Put 11 here.
  • {% ui "Lot submission ends" %}: an hour or two before the start, so nobody's typing in lots while you set up. You can still add lots for people after it closes.
  • The fees. "$1 + 30%" is common. A 10% pre-registration discount gets sellers doing their own typing.
  • Charging members less? Set up the alternate split.

The unsold lot fee is only charged on lots you end unsold on {% page "auction_lot_winners_dynamic" "Set lot winners" %}. A lot the seller left at home, or that never reached the table, costs nothing.

{% aitip %}Ask "what are the settings for this auction?" or "make the minimum bid $1".{% endaitip %}

3. Bidders and bidder numbers

Everyone who buys or sells needs to be on the {% page "auction_tos_list" "Users" %} tab. People who join through the site add themselves. Everyone else, you add.{% if help.site.in_person_join_on_day %} At in-person auctions here, {{ help.site.in_person_join_on_day }}% of people join on the day.{% endif %}

  1. Type their name in the search box on the Users tab. Already there? You're done.
  2. If not, click {% ui "Create user" %} under the empty search. It fills in the name you typed.
  3. As you type, the site offers the email, phone and address from your past auctions. Check it's the right person.
  4. Only a name is required. An email is worth having: it's how they get their invoice.
{% tip %}Always search first. It's what stops the same person ending up in the auction twice, with two bidder numbers and two invoices.{% endtip %}

Adding lots of people at once

Open the menu next to {% ui "Add user" %}:

  • {% page "bulk_add_users" "Bulk add users" %}: type them in, one per row. {% ui "Import from old auctions" %} brings last year's people, with the same bidder numbers.
  • {% ui "Import from CSV" %}: a spreadsheet with columns named Email, Name, Bidder number, Phone, Address, Club member, Allow bidding, Memo and Admin. Only the ones you have. Importing again updates people; nobody is deleted.
  • {% page "import_from_google_drive" "Set up Google Drive sync" %}: the same columns in a Google Sheet shared as "anyone with the link can view". {% page "sync_google_drive" "Sync" %} pulls in changes whenever you like.

Bidder numbers

They're handed out automatically.

  • Paddles: hand them out from the top of the pile and change each person's number to match. Or import your roster with a Bidder number column.
  • Same number every time: a person keeps their number in all your auctions.
  • Words work: Initials, or first names at a small auction where everyone knows everyone.
  • Keep them three digits. This helps reduce typos: a bid recorder isn't likely to think 819 is a sell price. Unless your club sells better fish than mine, anyway.
  • The list sorts numbers as text, so 10 comes before 9.
{% tip %}You can change someone's bidder number at any time, even halfway through an auction. Lot numbers don't change, since they're already printed on labels. Don't worry, the invoice will still be accurate.{% endtip %}

Duplicates happen anyway. Type "duplicate" in the search box. Keep the one with the invoice, and use Merge with... in the other one's {% ui "Actions" %} menu.

4. Helpers and auction admins

  1. Your helper makes an account and joins the auction: {% ui "Join auction" %} at the bottom of the auction page.
  2. On the Users tab, click their name and tick {% ui "Grant admin permissions to help run this auction" %}.

Admins can do everything you can, all at once on their own devices. {% page "auction_history" "Admin history" %}, under {% ui "More" %}, shows who changed what.

5. Getting lots in

The best lots are the ones sellers add themselves before the day. They get the pre-registration discount, the labels are already on the bags, and people browse them at home{% if help.site.self_added_premium %}. On this site they sell for about {{ help.site.self_added_premium }}% more than lots typed in at the desk{% endif %}. Send sellers Taking part in an in-person auction.

For everyone else, you add their lots: {% ui "Add lots" %} next to their name on the Users tab. {% page "bulk_add_lots_auto" "The add lots page" %} is a grid, one lot per row, that saves as you type. Type the name, press Tab, move on. A good typist does ten lots a minute.

{% mike %}I am not a good typist, this is why my club offers a 20% preregistration discount.{% endmike %}
  • Check the name at the top before you start. Lots can't be moved to a different person afterwards.
  • Donations and vendor lots: a user for each vendor ("Main Street Pets donation"), with no email, so you know what each gave.
  • Need a description or quantity? {% ui "Full editor" %} on a row opens the whole lot.
  • A spreadsheet of lots? {% page "import_lots_from_csv" "Import lots from CSV" %}, on the Lots tab: Name, Email, Lot Name, Description, Quantity, Reserve Price, Buy Now Price, Category, Breeder Points and Donation.
{% tip %}Don't put numbers in lot names. "3 Apistos" is fine; "Lot 12 Apistos" will confuse people.{% endtip %} {% mike %}Mike came in at 11:59 with 20 bags, 3 of them leaking, and wanted them all added to the auction. Mike is the reason I added the pre-registration discount feature, even though he still doesn't use it.{% endmike %} {% aitip %}Ask "add 12 lots called fish for bidder 14".{% endaitip %}

6. Labels

A printed label on every bag saves more time than anything else here. Lot labels covers printers and paper. In short:

  • Sellers who pre-register print their own.
  • You print the rest from {% page "auction_printing" "Print labels" %} under {% ui "More" %}, or per person from {% ui "Print labels" %} on the Users tab.

7. Check-in at the door

Run two lines: people who registered, who just need their number, and walk-ins, who need adding. Big auctions add a third for sellers with lots to add.

Look each person up on the Users tab and hand them a card or paddle with their number.

If you manage your club through this site, you can sync your auctions with your members list.
  • {% page "auction_quick_check_in" "Checkin" %}: scan a member's card with a phone camera or a USB scanner.
  • {% page "auction_self_check_in" "Self-checkin" %}, under {% ui "More" %}: a laptop and scanner by the door, and members scan their own card.
  • People with the phone app can check themselves in as they arrive, if the rules allow it.
  • A guest with no card: add them as a new user, which checks them in.

Barcodes and scanners has which scanners work and what else they can do. With check-in on, {% page "auction_door_prizes" "Door prizes" %} picks a random checked-in person for each prize.

{% aitip %}Ask "check in bidder 14", or "who hasn't checked in?"{% endaitip %}

8. Selling: the auctioneer's table

Your recorder sits next to the auctioneer with {% page "auction_lot_winners_dynamic" "Set lot winners" %} open (under {% ui "More" %}). For every lot:

  1. Type the lot number. The photo and name come up, so you know it's the right bag.
  2. Type the price.
  3. Type the winner's bidder number.
  4. Press Enter.

The page catches the usual mistakes before they reach an invoice, like a lot number that doesn't exist or a bidder nobody has. {% ui "Undo" %} takes back the last one.

The lot queue

{% page "auction_lot_queue" "Lot queue" %}, under {% ui "More" %}, is the running order. A runner adds each bag as it comes to the table: a USB scanner on the lot's QR code, {% ui "Scan with camera" %} on a phone, or the lot number and {% ui "Add" %}.

The lot marked {% ui "Selling now" %} is on the block. The queue moves on by itself:

  • Record that lot on Set lot winners, sold or {% ui "End lot unsold" %}, and the next lot moves up, skipping any after it that are already recorded.
  • Set lot winners fills in the next lot number for you. Each recorder works down the queue at their own pace, so a second recorder, or one catching up from paper slips, never loses their place.
  • A lot sold out of turn is recorded as usual, and the queue stays where the room is.

By hand:

Next, BackMove the queue by one lot. On a projector, the arrow keys or a presentation clicker do the same. I can't think of a time you'd need this feature, but it's there because people asked for it.
Drag Reorder the lots still to come.
Take a lot out.
Scan it againA lot that didn't sell goes back on the end, for another go.

With {% rule "message_users_when_lots_sell" %} on, people watching a lot get a notification when it's one of the next ten, and another when it's selling. Without the queue, the notification goes out when the bid recorder types the lot number, so type it in when bidding starts, not after.

Bumps

Plenty of clubs let a buyer pay a few dollars to move a lot up the queue. Every club does it differently, so there's no button for it. An AI agent can do it for you. Tell it your club's rule once, or save it as a skill, then say:

Bump lot 5 for bidder 325.

The agent finds bidder 325's invoice, adds a $3 bump fee as an adjustment, and moves lot 5 to the top of the queue.

Keeping the room

About two lots a minute is a good pace. Prices drop as the day goes on and people leave.

{% if help.stats_auction and not help.stats_auction.is_online and help.stats.lots_per_hour %}{% helptip "graph-up" %}{{ help.stats_auction.title }} sold about {{ help.stats.lots_per_hour }} lots an hour{% if help.stats.late_price_drop %}, and lots in the last third of the day sold for {{ help.stats.late_price_drop }}% less than in the first{% endif %}.{% endhelptip %}{% endif %}
  • Swap in a fresh auctioneer.
  • Put dry goods on a silent auction table instead of selling them out loud.
  • Use buy now prices.
  • Save the door prizes for later in the day.
{% aitip %}Ask "lot 12 sold to bidder 14 for $8", "bump lot 5 for bidder 325", or "draw a door prize".{% endaitip %}

9. Checkout and payment

Open {% page "auction_quick_checkout" "Checkout" %} under {% ui "More" %}. Type a bidder number or name. Their total comes up: what they owe, or what the club owes them, and how many unsold lots they're taking home. Take the money, click {% ui "Paid" %}, next.

  • Card payments: with Square connected, the screen shows a QR code. The buyer pays on their own phone and the invoice marks itself paid. With the phone app, {% ui "Tap to Pay" %} takes the card on yours. See Card payments.
  • Cash or check: type "cash" or "check #1041" in the invoice's {% ui "Memo" %} box. Only admins see it.
  • T-shirts, raffle tickets, memberships: open the invoice, {% ui "Adjustments" %}, {% ui "Charge extra" %}. Or scan a barcode for them.
{% tip %}{% ui "Paid" %} is the button you want. {% ui "Ready" %} means "please pay", and marking someone ready who already paid sends them an email that reads like a bill.{% endtip %}

Clicking Paid emails the person their invoice as a receipt, if they have an email.

A seller who wants to leave early: take what they owe for what they bought, add a {% ui "Discount" %} for that amount ("paid before leaving"), and leave the invoice open. When their lots sell, it shows what the club owes them.

{% tip %}If this happens often at your auctions, this is another great place to add an AI skill.{% endtip %} {% aitip %}Ask "bidder 14 paid", "who still owes?", or "take $5 off Jane's invoice".{% endaitip %}

Letting people bid online too

{% if enable_help %}{% include 'youtube_embed.html' with videoId=hybrid_tutorial chapters=hybrid_tutorial_chapters %}{% endif %}

Turn on {% rule "online_bidding" %} and people can bid on the site before and during the auction.

{% tip %}For most clubs it's more trouble than it's worth: the regulars wait and bid in the room, and a lot that sells in the room below its online bid is an error you have to override.{% endtip %} {% if help.is_admin and not help.auction.is_online %}{% helptip %}{{ help.auction.title }}: {{ help.auction.get_online_bidding_display }}.{% endhelptip %}{% endif %}

Where it does work:

  • A silent auction for dry goods. Put them on a table with labels. People scan a label's QR code and bid on their phones. At the end, search the Lots tab for them and click {% ui "Sell lots to online high bidder" %}. A custom checkbox called "Silent auction" makes them easy to find.
  • Pre-sales. Set online bidding to buy now only, and require a buy now price. People buy before the day, and those lots never reach the auctioneer.

Set lot winners shows the online high bidder as you type each lot number. On the Lots tab, "hasbids" in the search box finds lots with online bids, and "qrviewed" finds lots whose winner scanned the label.

Things that go wrong on the day

The internetHall wifi is often bad. A phone hotspot runs a laptop or two. The phone app keeps the Users, add lots and Set lot winners pages working with no connection. Keep a paper tally sheet anyway.
A lot sold to the wrong personType it again on Set lot winners with {% ui "Ignore errors and save" %}.
A lot sold, then turned out dead or wrongOn the lot, {% ui "Admin actions" %}, {% ui "Remove or refund" %}, with a percentage.
A seller brought back labels for lots that diedRemove those lots, so they aren't charged the unsold fee. This is why my club doesn't charge an unsold lot fee.
An invoice has lots somebody didn't buyAlmost always a mistyped bidder number. Search the Lots tab for "winner:" and their number.
A shipment of donated lots never arrivedIf each shipment is under its own user, {% ui "Problems" %} in that user's {% ui "Actions" %} menu finds every lot and buyer to refund.
{% aitip %}Ask "undo the last sale", or "undo that".{% endaitip %}

After the auction

Pay the sellers. Most clubs mail a check within a week or two, to the address on the seller's invoice. Filter the Users tab by {% ui "Club owes" %}. No address? Ask before you write the check. Why there's no payout button.
