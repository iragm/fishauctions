Failure — forms people gave up on

Two different failures, and on this site the second is the bigger one. Rejected is the server refusing a submission, which is rare here on purpose: nearly every field is optional and most of the rest are filled in on save, so there is not much left to refuse. Abandoned is somebody editing the form and leaving without saving — the shape giving up actually takes, and completely invisible to the server until the page started reporting it. A form with rejections and no abandonments is one people can see how to fill in and keep getting wrong. One with abandonments and no rejections is one they cannot see how to fill in at all, and no validator will ever say so.

Buyers — how far people get

One row per auction, over that auction's whole life rather than the window above: people arrive weeks before they pay, and a funnel cut at 30 days reports the ones who paid last month as a drop-off. Every stage counts people, and somebody who never signed in counts as one — that is what this phase was for. Bid is blank for an in-person auction, where the bidding happens in a room and leaves no row until somebody wins. Joined includes the people an organizer typed in at the door, so it can be larger than the stages above it.
