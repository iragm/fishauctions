Donation tracking terms and conditions

These terms apply to donation tracking specifically. They're in addition to this site's terms and conditions, which still apply to everything else. By switching donation tracking on, your club accepts them.

Your club is the sender

Every donation email is your club's message, asking on your club's behalf, whether it leaves from this site or you copy it into your own email program. This site provides the tools; it does not solicit donations, is not a party to any arrangement you reach with a vendor, and takes no share of what you're given.

Following the law is your job

Asking businesses and the public for donations is regulated, and the rules vary by state. Some states require a club to be a registered non-profit, or to register as a charitable solicitor, before asking at all. Others have rules about raffles specifically. Your club is responsible for knowing which rules apply to it and following them, including US federal email law (CAN‑SPAM) and any state equivalent.

Don't describe your club as a registered charity, or a donation as tax deductible, unless that's true of your club and you're prepared to back it up. The email this site writes for you is instructed never to claim either on its own.

What you type about other people

You're responsible for the vendor names, addresses and notes you enter, for having a legitimate reason to contact each vendor, and for keeping what you write about them accurate and civil. Don't upload lists you bought, scraped, or were given for another purpose.

Opting out is permanent

Every donation email carries an unsubscribe link. When a vendor uses it, that address is blocked for every club on this site, immediately and permanently, and there is no way for you or for us to put them back. Don't work around it by adding the same business under a different address.

Automatically written email

When a language model drafts a request or summarizes a reply, the result is a starting point and can be wrong. Read it before you send it. What you send is what you approved, not what the model wrote. Summaries and statuses attached to incoming replies are a convenience, not a record of what a vendor actually said — the original message is kept, and it's the one that counts.

Limits, and no guarantees

Donation tracking is offered as-is, with no warranty of any kind. Email may be delayed, rejected, filtered as spam, or lost, and replies may never reach this site. Daily limits apply and may change. This site may suspend or withdraw the feature, for one club or for everyone, at any time and without notice — including where it looks like it's being used to send bulk unsolicited mail.
