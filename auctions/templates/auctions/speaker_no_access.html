Speaker directory

The speaker directory is shared by the member clubs of the Northeast Council of Aquarium Societies. It's open to anyone who holds a permission in an NEC member club, and your account doesn't have one yet.

If your club is an NEC member, ask one of your club's admins to give you a permission on the club's member list — any permission is enough to see the directory. If your club has joined the NEC recently and isn't marked as a member here yet, let us know and we'll flag it.
