Club health

A club is judged against its own cadence, not a global cutoff: a club that runs monthly and has missed two is in trouble, and a club that runs one big auction a year is fine at eight months. One threshold gets both wrong, which is probably why nobody trusted Club.active enough to maintain it. Rebuilt nightly.
