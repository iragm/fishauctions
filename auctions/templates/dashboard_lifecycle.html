Lifecycle

Everybody who is not running the auction: the buyer with no account, the seller who never opens a lot page, and the person whose first contact with this site is an invoice email. These are milestones, not a funnel — most of them have no order, so a person who did it differently is not a drop-out. Bidding is left out on purpose: it happens in about 5% of auctions. The last milestone is a paid invoice, which every auction has.
